# Lens Language

## 24mm

Use for:
- environmental portrait
- travel photography
- strong spatial depth

Keywords:
wide perspective, foreground exaggeration, immersive space

## 35mm

Use for:
- documentary portrait
- Japanese lifestyle photography
- natural storytelling

Keywords:
humanistic, observational, realistic

## 50mm

Use for:
- classic portrait
- balanced perspective

Keywords:
natural compression, timeless portrait

## 85mm

Use for:
- cinematic portrait
- beauty photography

Keywords:
soft background separation, emotional close portrait
