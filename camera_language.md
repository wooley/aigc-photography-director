# Camera Language Reference

## Camera Spatial Model

A professional prompt should describe where the camera exists in the physical scene.

## Position Examples

### Observer View

Camera positioned far away, watching the subject from an architectural viewpoint.

Use for:
- loneliness
- storytelling
- environmental portrait

### Tracking View

Camera follows the subject from side or rear direction.

Use for:
- youth photography
- documentary feeling
- movement

### Intimate Portrait View

Camera close to shoulder or face level.

Use for:
- emotional portrait
- character study

## Lens Reference

### 24mm

Wide environmental perspective.
Strong depth and spatial relationship.

### 35mm

Natural documentary perspective.
Ideal for lifestyle photography.

### 50mm

Classic human perspective.
Balanced portrait compression.

### 85mm

Cinematic portrait lens.
Strong background separation.

## Composition Variables

Always define:

- subject percentage in frame
- left/right placement
- foreground objects
- depth layers
- negative space
