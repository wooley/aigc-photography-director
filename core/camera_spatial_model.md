# Camera Spatial Model

A photographic prompt must define spatial relationships.

## Seven Variables

Camera Position
× Camera Height
× Distance
× Direction
× Shot Size
× Foreground Relationship
× Subject Placement

## Shot Schema

```yaml
camera_position:
camera_height:
distance_to_subject:
direction:
lens:
shot_size:
foreground:
subject_position:
lighting:
color_profile:
```

Example:

Camera position: standing at the top of a harbor staircase.
Camera height: 3 meters above subject.
Distance: 15 meters.
Direction: downward 45 degree angle.
Lens: 35mm documentary lens.
Subject: occupies 15 percent of frame.
Foreground: rails create depth layers.
