# Reference Reverse Engineering Examples

## Example A: Chinese Ancient Garden Portrait

Input:

A Hanfu portrait in an oriental garden.

Analysis:

Camera:
50mm portrait lens, eye-level, 5 meters distance.

Composition:
Subject placed on right third, garden path creates leading lines.

Foreground:
Blurred bamboo leaves create depth.

Lighting:
Soft morning sunlight through trees.

Color:
Warm oriental cinematic portrait.

---

## Example B: American Sports Scene

Input:

Basketball player during competition.

Analysis:

Camera:
24mm wide angle from sideline floor level.

Composition:
Low angle, athlete dominates frame.

Foreground:
Shoes and court markings enter foreground.

Lighting:
Indoor stadium contrast.

Color:
Sports documentary realism.

---

## Example C: Japanese Fashion Street Photography

Input:

Tokyo street fashion portrait.

Analysis:

Camera:
35mm film camera following from side-back direction.

Composition:
Natural walking moment, subject occupies 40 percent.

Foreground:
Glass reflection and pedestrians create layers.

Lighting:
Overcast urban soft light.

Color:
Fuji film simulation, subtle grain.
