# Series Generator

## Principle

A photo series should vary camera language, not only locations.

Bad:

10 images = 10 backgrounds

Good:

10 images = 10 photographic perspectives

## Shot Variation Matrix

01 Establishing wide shot
02 Low angle dynamic shot
03 Side-follow documentary shot
04 Shoulder close-up
05 Foreground obstruction shot
06 Backlight silhouette
07 Reflection composition
08 85mm portrait climax
09 High angle emotional shot
10 Candid closing moment

## Consistency

Keep stable:
- identity
- character design
- color system
- visual era

Change:
- camera
- composition
- pose
- movement
- interaction with environment
